## 1. EATING VERBS (20 verbs)

**Definition**: Consumption of solid food through mastication and swallowing.

### Intensity/Speed Spectrum

**Rapid/Aggressive Eating**
- devour, gobble, bolt, wolf down, pig out

**Standard Eating**
- eat, consume, ingest, dine, feed

**Slow/Cautious Eating**
- nibble, pick at, peck, snack

**Pleasurable Eating**
- feast, banquet, gourmet, savor, taste

**Repetitive Chewing Actions**
- chew, masticate, munch, gnaw, crunch

**Continuous/Grazing**
- graze, browse, feed, forage

### Interconnectivity Leaders
| Verb | Interconnections | Characteristics |
|------|------------------|-----------------|
| consume | 10 | highest density; most synonymic variety |
| gulp | 7 | rapid swallowing action |
| munch | 5 | repeated chewing with sound |
| ingest | 5 | physiological intake |
| eat | 5 | prototypical eating verb |

### Causal Chains
- Hunger + food availability → eat → satiation → energy gain
- Appetite → bite → chew → swallow → consumption
- Greed → gorge → overeat → fullness → discomfort
- Excitement → devour → rapid consumption → satisfaction

### Key Antonym Pairs
- eat ↔ fast (starvation abstinence)
- feast ↔ fast (celebration vs. restraint)
- gorge ↔ diet (indulgence vs. control)

|------|------------------|---------|
