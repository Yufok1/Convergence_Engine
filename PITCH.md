# 🧬 Seeking Collaboration: Neuroevolutionary Autonomous Agents

**Project:** Convergence Engine

---

## Overview

I'm developing a neuroevolutionary system that breeds autonomous agents through survival pressure rather than hand-coded behaviors. Thousands of neural organisms compete, cooperate, form alliances, and develop emergent communication—only the fittest survive. The surviving "brain" is exported as a deployable model (cocoon) for real-world autonomous systems.

---

## Technical Stack

- DQN-based neural decision making with genetic crossover
- Multi-agent gym environments with adversarial selection (Highlander tournaments)
- Emergent language system with semantic grounding
- Alliance/warfare mechanics driving cooperative evolution
- Currently running 2,000+ organisms at scale on cloud compute

---

## Applications

Autonomous swarms for GPS/comms-denied environments, disaster response, agricultural monitoring, infrastructure inspection, and space/subsea exploration.
